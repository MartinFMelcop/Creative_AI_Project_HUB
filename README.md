## Try the current scene

**[Virgo Diamond: The North Star](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/north-star/README.md)** is the principal product:
an original MMORPG centered on recognizable character identity and meaningful adventures.
**Unity/C# is selected for production, Windows is the priority platform, and Steam is
intended distribution.** The current downloadable experiment is a local C#/browser scene.

### The coil and the shutters

Edda has five shutter crates to secure before four shifts of light end. A coil also
needs a far-bank handoff. Rill keeps the line secured until you survey the actual route
and hear his current offer. Signing reserves his help; departure costs daylight.
A completed handoff does not erase unfinished local work.

Extract the complete trusted ZIP and follow its platform guide. Both include a private
runtime, notices and recoverable source; SDK, Node and checkout are unnecessary for
recipients. Accepted choices resume from a local save outside the immutable payload.
Do not elevate or bypass execution/HTTP policies to make the experiment run.

Neither recipient is a Unity player or Steam delivery. Licensed Editor/modules,
production Windows acceptance, independent player/device assessment and commercial
clearance remain unresolved. These are experimental scene assets and fiction under
Martin's direction, not approved world canon or an externally validated MMORPG.

The [three-brain library](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/brains/README.md) preserves five supplied documents and
234 chapters. Specialized agents use selective consultation; documents are not agents,
model training or approval. [Protected historical sources](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/references/README.md)
retain separate rights, variants and integrity contracts. Technical reuse does not
import fictional universes; there is no blanket commercial license for all material.
