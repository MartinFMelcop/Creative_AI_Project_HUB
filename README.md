# Creative AI Project HUB

A **Design-Driven technology portfolio developed with generative AI**, directed by
**[Martin Melcop Neto](https://github.com/MartinFMelcop)**. Inspect the code,
creative resources, design decisions, experimental instruments and execution
records behind the demonstrations.

[![Lab checks](https://github.com/MartinFMelcop/Creative_AI_Project_HUB/actions/workflows/lab-checks.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/MartinFMelcop/Creative_AI_Project_HUB/actions/workflows/lab-checks.yml)

**[Virgo Diamond: The North Star](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/north-star/README.md)** is the principal
product: an original MMORPG in which character identity becomes visible through
choices, builds, classes, professions and achievements. Its current stage is
`local_prototype`: one playable destination consumer, 14 cataloged capabilities and
NS-C03/NS-C04/NS-C07 adapted. Godot is the prototype tool; the production engine remains undecided.
The portfolio makes existing technology inspectable while applied laboratories
investigate what can actually serve that experience.

## Playable North Star increment

[Crossing Drill](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/north-star/prototype/README.md)
lets two experimental approaches protect the same moving courier: frontal interception
or redirection/mobility. At the first marker, a medicine case falls south of the road.
Keep escorting, or leave the line to recover it and hand it to the courier. Held nearby
interaction takes time and prevents combat actions; the courier and threats keep moving.
The two choices have distinct inspectable outcomes. Optional rehearsal, causal review,
OPEN range/time and earned cosmetic keepsakes remain available. F1 now remaps all twelve
gameplay keys with explicit apply/discard, matching hints and recoverable local preferences.
The previously silent encounter now has distinct threat and character-response cues,
with optional mute and retained visual warnings. Guardian now chooses sustained Steady
guard or short-window Riposte before the attempt; the latter risks expiry for a longer
real counter opening. Rehearsal uses the chosen technique. Original shield/blade actors
now show accepted actions and local contact/recovery; the sentinel opens with its real
vulnerability window. Three real close counters can now disable it: threats stop and
the courier runs. Defending without counters remains a complete route; the gate still
requires a living courier and player. F1/H and F1/I now offer independent session-only
single-press guard and medicine interaction; costs/commitment stay live and interruption
disarms active intentions. Hold remains the default.

The editable Godot project and small engine-dependent PCK execute the same consumer.
An optional [Linux x86_64 player](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/north-star/prototype/dist/crossing-drill-linux-0.2.0.zip)
now bundles its official runtime: extract the whole folder and play without an editor,
SDK or checkout. [Delivery evidence](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/evidence/north-star-gamepad/RUN.json)
includes actual release execution, default-window input and save/reentry. It establishes
delivery on the tested Linux environment, not other platforms or human enjoyment.
[Native/input evidence](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/evidence/north-star-courier-conversation/RUN.json)
and the [living design record](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/north-star/DESIGN.md)
separate fixed-policy feasibility and agent visual assessment from still-missing human
understanding, identity and enjoyment evidence.

![A wounded character works a gate catch while the winch remains threatened](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/evidence/north-star-return-gate/guardian-working.png)

An optional **F2 roadside workshop** now connects crafting to the crossing: walk to a
bench, make and wear a slower six-health coat or faster four-health wrap. Recycle
before deployment or keep standard clothing. Both approaches use the real fitting;
no permanent profession, economy or saved power is implied.

At a nearby sign, choose the direct road or a longer upper route before the courier
commits. The actual detour adds distance and raises a partial bolt filter; ground
pulses and nearer contacts remain dangerous. Route choice alone cannot complete the
escort. Source/PCK controls and real keyboard navigation demonstrate the decision;
fixed policies and agent image review do not establish player preference or balance.

After a real escort win, **F2 continues into a second playable objective**. Actual
remaining health and fitting carry into two separated catches, a threatened stationary
winch and an exit that must be crossed alive. Work prevents combat; protecting first or
working under threat has observable costs. Without aid, retry restores arrival state, not full health.
The courier stays safe; no extra reward or account progression is invented.

**F3 on selection** now lets you choose skin, hair and cloth dye independently from
approach and fitted gear. Preview actual actions, apply or discard, then see the same
look through both goals. These free choices last for the session and change no combat
values. The workflow makes expression usable; player-recognizable identity remains untested.

![Shared actor preview in the native character-look editor](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/evidence/north-star-character-look/editor-rest.png)

**F4 after arrival** opens an optional [conversation with the courier](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/north-star/prototype/README.md#talk-to-the-courier).
Ask about the actual crossing, then allocate one offered repair strip: prepare one
health point for yourself, brace the return winch, or leave it with the courier.
Select and confirm deliberately; only the confirmed choice reaches the next objective.
Retry restores that preparation without duplicating it. Direct F2 continuation remains.
This is an executed narrative-to-gameplay choice, not evidence of player attachment,
final quest canon, account progression or persistent social relationships.

## Applied research

The [combat-response pilot](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/labs/combat-response/README.md) uses P06's actual
Godot encounter as a controlled timing/response instrument. It investigates
whether guard, dodge and no response have distinct contact and recovery
consequences under repeatable inputs. It supplies bounded technical evidence for
future character-action research; it does not implement Guardian/Duelist classes,
multiplayer, MMORPG scale or independent player evaluation.

The [recorded 28-case matrix](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/evidence/combat-response/results.json),
repeated twice, distinguishes a close parry's early counter from a dodge's
positional escape. Early/late guards, inward dodges, range, exhausted resources
and an overlapping hazard expose different failure cases. These fixed traces
do not compare each response's best possible policy. From the maintained lab,
with Python 3.11+ and Godot 4.6.3, reproduce and compare them:

```sh
python3 labs/combat-response/run.py --godot godot --output /tmp/combat-response.json --compare evidence/combat-response/results.json
```

The [origin inventory](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/labs/origins.json) records all eleven origins,
dependencies, existing consumers, preservation risks and extraction work.
**One pilot is implemented.** Other laboratory destinations are candidates.
P01–P11 are retained technology resources in controlled transition, with no
requirement to keep releasing eleven independent products. Their useful code,
media, tests, reading editions and executable demonstrations remain available
through the [origin index](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/projects/README.md).

## Knowledge and authorial direction

The [three-brain library](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/brains/README.md) contains the exact supplied
Engineering, Scientific and Creative volumes: **234 chapters**, a consultation
catalog and collection index. A deterministic local Python query retrieves
selected complete chapters. Compact discovery and explained bilingual exploration now
have [reproducible retrieval evidence](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/evidence/brain-retrieval/RUN.json),
with strict defaults and original source bytes preserved. The brains inform engineering, experimental and
creative decisions; they are not agents, executable commands or model training.
Their source texts require specific authorization to change.

[Derived knowledge](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/knowledge/README.md) stores contextual observations,
interpretations and limits from executed work. It is distinct from the durable
brain volumes and the [preserved historical source library](martin-creative-ai-lab-v0.2.0/martin-creative-ai-lab/references/README.md).
The Creative Brain guides Virgo Diamond; author decisions, editorial directions,
hypotheses and verified behavior retain different status. Reusing a technique
from an origin does not merge its fiction into the MMORPG.
