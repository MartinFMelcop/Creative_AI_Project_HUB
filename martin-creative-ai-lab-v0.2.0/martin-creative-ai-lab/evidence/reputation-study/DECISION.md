## An explicit original consumer policy

[Room](../../north-star/reputation-study/Host/Room.cs) owns one ephemeral original trial
with four work periods and three coins. Its Quay and Orchard counters independently
start at an explicitly experimental-1. This is a local assay setting, not a judgment of
the player, inherited worth, approved faction design or universal social trust.

Borrowing creates custody of that counter's single measuring tool and no standing.
Only an accepted inspection-and-return event with that custody and two available work
periods clears held custody and applies the selected+2 arithmetic. Another counter's
held tool, a promise, read, redraw or elapsed wall time cannot supply the event. The
other counter receives no improvement. The command is an authored state transition;
it does not establish physical human inspection or real-world service delivery.

Minor help costs one actual trial period and applies+1. Four paid minor helps from-1
still leave standing-1 and exhaust time. That losing policy is retained as a counterexample
of the selected source rule, not silently retuned into an enjoyable or fair policy.
Minor help after an actual crossing to+1 can improve standing at its real cost.

This original service requires standing at least+1, two coins and one work period. Only
the accepted service transition debits those resources and records a terminal receipt.
Repeated service or return cannot charge, reward or receipt twice. An outstanding loan
in the other region can remain held when time is insufficient; expiry does not return
it. Service is the terminal experimental outcome, not later gameplay consumption or an
implemented historical effect catalog.
