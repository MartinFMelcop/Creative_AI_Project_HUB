Read current living design, FieldJourney Room/Case/web contracts, Brain consultation

## Selected consequence and alternatives

The existing Field journey has support withdrawal at a workbench but no deliberate
whole-expedition exit after paid departure. Ordinary continuation or defeat are the
only route closures. An explicit irreversible local withdrawal changes available
future actions and cargo custody without inventing child defeat, home arrival or
victory. This is a coherent product improvement after two laboratory units, not a
new source capability or another service/matrix/application.

CRE-005's agency direction materially requires explicit cost and readable consequence:
spent rigs/materials and received damage remain; previously earned XP remains earned;
carried sample is forfeited; no future action can resume this ephemeral expedition.
Withdrawal must not be conflated with the existing unpaid workshop-support choice.
CRE-055's contextual counterplay proposal is a hypothesis, not permission to import
class, region, creature or boss canon. It favors a different response to risk over
adding HP or another attack variant. This experiment does not establish that quitting
is balanced, preferred, comprehensible or an optimal policy.

Other credible frontiers are actual licensed Unity production and appropriate people/
devices, authorable field interaction, or keyboard controls. They are not rejected
collections. The selected gap is observable in current code and can be implemented
without editing frozen Session/PreparedExpedition, new packaging or persistence.

## Owned UI implementation

Only existing `web/index.html`, `web/app.js` and `web/style.css` were edited.
The new button says “Withdraw expedition · forfeit carried sample”; nearby text states
that closure is irreversible and is not workshop-support withdrawal. It is hidden
before actual departure, after actual completion and after accepted withdrawal.

The server's exact `withdrawn` flag drives a distinct visible receipt/summary. The road
says EXPEDITION WITHDRAWN and the encounter says WITHDRAWN / HISTORICAL ENCOUNTER.
Actual child HP/resolve/beat/position/XP remain visible as historical facts. The actor
is visually subdued; active threat and sampling-site pictures hide. No raw child
Active/Won/Complete value is forged to obtain that presentation. Original normal
renderer branches and factual captions remain untouched outside the withdrawal branch.

All mutating buttons, inputs and selects are disabled after withdrawal while explicit
Read remains available. The sample loss caption distinguishes forfeiture from historical
acquisition and does not erase its actual collection receipt. Unused resources are not
silently confiscated or refunded. This is a trusted-local observer, not a security
boundary; the actual owner must independently refuse later commands.

Static `node --check` and `git diff --check` passed. Science owns actual browser controls,
negative renderers and captures; this record alone does not claim their execution or
human perception. Agent design/review is not author approval or commercial clearance.

## Revision after first actual execution

The first browser execution passed21 controls, but final semantic review identified
that the existing generic lost-case SVG depicts cracks. The new withdrawal forfeits
custody without observing physical breakage. A technically passing software run did
not justify that picture. After Science confirmed its run and hosts had ended, the
renderer was narrowly corrected: actual `loss_reason=withdrawn` hides the cracked
picture and displays the dashed custody outline, with a FORFEITED label and explicit
accessible title. Ordinary combat-loss and unused-case pictures remain unchanged.
Science owns the added computed-visibility acceptance and fresh final execution.

The older app/CSS producer preimages were reconstructed by reversing only these
three known edits and verified byte-identical against both recorded browser-01 input
hashes. `creative-preimages/recovery.json` records that this recovery is not another
execution. The first execution, captures and exact producer remain superseded evidence.
