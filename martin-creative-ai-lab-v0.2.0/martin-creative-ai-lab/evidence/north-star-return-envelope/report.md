# Return encounter envelope

Fixed policies at 60 Hz; initial idle delay is a controlled intervention, not human reaction time. Loss does not prove no feasible policy exists. Repetitions and deliveries are not independent participants. No enjoyment, balance, physical input, release-template or production-engine claim.

## Actual observations

540 conditions, 2 identical takes per delivery; source/PCK equality required.

W = policy won; L = policy lost; T = observation budget exhausted. Cells show HP / winch at stop. These are deterministic conditions, not success probabilities.
