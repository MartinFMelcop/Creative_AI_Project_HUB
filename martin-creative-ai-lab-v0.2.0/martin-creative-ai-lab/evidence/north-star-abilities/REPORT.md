## Selection and contribution

Existing remote preparation already exposes real choices, but adds no ability.
A larger network stack lacks a demonstrated immediate ownership requirement;
independent player/author observation remains valuable but unavailable here. Waystone
already has a complete consequential afternoon campaign and Tokyo 80s two playable
stages with review/rehearsal; extending those is credible, without a blocking need
that outweighs North Star's missing behavioral build experiment in this execution.
Unity was not found on PATH; this does not establish its absence from every possible
installation, and no Unity execution or production engine selection is claimed.

The selected contribution is a complete, separately runnable **ability production
consumer**, not an expansion of the catalog/preparation HUD alone. Two original
experimental profiles change player movement, payment and action timing. One shared
native data contract serves the playable preparation and read-only candidate execution.
The main adventure, developer authority and distributed PCK/Linux player are unchanged.
This preserves an inspectable experiment without silently adopting new class canon
or paying an unrelated distribution/network migration cost.

CRE-013 (H) materially informs exclusive pre-entry choices with behavioral consequences;
ENG-015 informs an additive, narrow consumer seam; SCI-017 informs constructed matched
controls and retaining disadvantages. No new library, laboratory or transfer is claimed.

## Executed checks and observations

- **60 native controls**: schema/type/bounds/identity failures, missing data, exact
  single payment, locked trajectory, conflicting action suppression, no travel recovery,
  action recovery, no replayed suppressed edges, pulse damage, field bounds, no guard/
  step bypass, locked selection/restart and actual View focus/preparation/resume/retry.
- **Ten matched opening fixtures** compare immediate strike, Snap, Reach, ordinary
  walking and the existing paid step. At 360 px / 0.7 s OPEN, only the prescribed Reach
  policy counters after recovery. At 170 px / 0.4 s OPEN, Snap, walk and step counter;
  Reach recovers too late. All misses remain in `native.json`. The setup explicitly
  assigns an opening/position; it does not claim these situations' natural frequency,
  optimal policies or that Snap improves on ordinary walking in the nearer fixture.
- **Three complete model journeys**, using the existing Guardian policy plus one
  initial selected rush, finish alive: baseline 5 HP / 3 cargo, Snap 5 / 3, Reach 4 / 3.
  Each selected rush really starts once. Baseline's entire snapshot matches the
  unchanged base model's ordinary journey. Reach's additional health loss is retained.
- **Three Python authoring regressions** execute changed data, malformed/oversized/
  wrong-version rejection and protected output paths. The retained candidate changes
  Snap speed to 500: actual travel becomes 60 px while spending the same 20 resolve.
  It remains a separate candidate; maintained source/data hashes are unchanged.
- **16 actual-window OS controls** exercise preparation, selection lock, entry,
  payment, visible committed travel, 240 px actual displacement, held-key nonrepeat,
  guard refusal, local focus-loss pause, frozen tick, explicit resume, fresh movement
  and clean close. These are XTest events, not independent people or physical devices.
  The initial rendered acknowledgement is bounded and can refocus/retry F12 during
  startup; it does not consume gameplay inputs or start the model.
- Agent inspected `preparation.png` and `committed.png`: selected trade-offs fit the
  finite screen, the cyan remaining trajectory is visible, and actual resolve/status
  accompany the world. Image inspection does not validate human readability/enjoyment.

Development checks caught an unsupported display format, hidden preparation pause and
an early View test before SceneTree readiness; corrected runs have no native errors.
An initial OS run omitted the rendered acknowledgement; the verifier now requires a
bounded actual rendered/input acknowledgement rather than assuming discovery is ready.
No isolated renderer root cause or general hardware reliability claim is made.

## Limits and continuity

This is a source-only Guardian experiment, with two supported rush profile slots and
one implemented operation. It does not author arbitrary skills, talent graphs or quests.
There is no shipped ability, multiplayer authority integration, independent human
validation, balance/competitive diversity conclusion, saved progression or maturity
promotion. No protected source, attribution/license, existing release, capability state
or historical negative evidence is changed. Adoption, further ability behavior and
independent author/player observation remain alternatives, not automatic next tasks.
