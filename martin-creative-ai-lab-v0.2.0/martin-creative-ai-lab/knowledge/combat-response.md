# Avoidance is not the same observation as a counter opportunity

**Question/context:** Can P06's parry, directional dodge and a single seal be treated
as equivalent defensive options when designing a bounded character comparison?
The [combat-response instrument](../labs/combat-response/README.md) drives unchanged
Veilbreak 0.6.0 at 60 Hz, one stationary phase-one sweep, 28 cases repeated twice.
See its [inputs](../labs/combat-response/scenarios.json),
[outcomes](../evidence/combat-response/results.json) and
[execution record](../evidence/combat-response/RUN.json) before applying the finding.

**Observed positive result:** a near parry 5/8 ticks before contact prevents sweep
damage and permits the fixed early blade counter for 24 damage. Outward dodges at
all five tested leads also prevent damage but end outside blade range. Early dodge
commitment rejects the common blade request. At 110px a parry succeeds while the
103px blade misses: successful defense does not imply a reachable reward.

**Observed negative controls:** no-resolve defenses are rejected; 111px guard fails;
a single seal does not interrupt this sweep; an imposed overlapping echo still hurts
after a successful parry. These refute general protection or equivalence of all
responses within these cases. They do not prove that one option dominates all play.

**Interpretation/practical consequence:** an instrument for recognizable action needs
to expose accepted/rejected commands, commitment, position, resource cost and reachable
consequence alongside survival. A damage-only summary would hide the positional and
temporal trade-off. Future character experiments should measure whether intended
rewards remain accessible after a valid response, rather than changing class labels
or adding attributes to indistinguishable actions.

**Validity limit:** this is a fixed early-counter probe, not the best policy for each
response or a full timing sweep. It excludes player perception, delayed counter choice,
other attacks/phases, networking, physical input, readability and listening. Repeated
traces are not independent participants. Class identity/fun/learning remain untested;
The later [crossing consumer](../north-star/prototype/README.md) applies this question
in a new geometric model; it does not retroactively extend this origin experiment. CRE-001/012/084 motivates the question,
not its empirical proof; ENG-004/015 and SCI-005/017/051 bound the inference.

**Revisit when:** a real character consumer, other threat, timing rate, movement policy
or external observation changes the envelope. Preserve the current trace as a scoped
reference, not a universal combat rule or automatically approved creative decision.

## Destination application — local crossing prototype

The new [consumer](../north-star/prototype/README.md) demonstrates distinct feasible
protection/redirection paths under one courier goal. Source inspection found that an
endpoint-only redirected beam could ignore an ally earlier on the segment. The actual
model now resolves the nearer eligible contact; the new negative case rejects a
controlled endpoint-only mutation. This is a destination control, not retroactive
proof of a defect in P06. Practical consequence: measure who owns an impact and who
bears the cost, rather than treating a changed target label as successful protection.
Scripted completion does not demonstrate human understanding or final class identity.


## Destination feedback — isolated rehearsal is not learning evidence

**Question/context:** Can a player inspect why a real response failed and retry it
without the next threat replacing the explanation? At crossing baseline88327a7, the
single transient message and terminal loss text erased that stated review target.
See [current execution](../evidence/north-star-rehearsal/RUN.json) and its retained
historical full-crossing comparison.

**Observation:** the same Combat consumer now supplies structured last-contact cause
and recovery advice. Native and actual synthetic-keyboard controls show explicit ready,
failed response, frozen review, clean retry and clean crossing handoff. Wrong facing,
feint without escape, ignored courier, pulse guard and distant counter cannot advance.
All four original crossing policy outcomes remain unchanged. Visual review found an
initial panel hiding the escaped character; the revised panel and last-contact outline
preserve the position needed to interpret the result.

**Interpretation/consequence:** keep causal feedback available at the moment of retry;
a global success counter or overwritten message cannot serve that recovery workflow.
Use role-appropriate advice and visible counter range/time in this consumer.

**Validity:** exercises reset resources/positions and supply the final opening. This
positive workflow result does not show sustained resource management, learning transfer,
unassisted comprehension or accessibility. Those remain inconclusive without relevant
people/devices. Revisit when such observation or encounter changes expose new failures.

**Input observation:** an actual XTest LEFT event was visible to logical key polling but
not physical-key polling in both baseline and new scene. The baseline kept facing right.
The corrected adapter uses logical identity for navigation arrows, physical positions
for WASD; native logical-only input plus actual wrong/right-facing trials pass. Core
combat outcomes remain unchanged. This is scoped Linux/Godot input evidence, not all
keyboard layouts or hardware accessibility.

## Destination agency — an optional task competes with escort position

**Question/context:** At baseline8c51b2f, choosing an approach changes defense, but the
only world objective remains an automatically advancing courier. Can a voluntary
recovery/handoff add observable action and consequence within that same encounter?
See [medicine execution](../evidence/north-star-medicine/RUN.json), its exact sources,
full route observations and controlled negative cases. ENG-004/005/023, SCI-017/062,
CRE-001/005 and CRE-047/058/086 informed requirements, ownership, controls and agency;
specific medicine fiction remains experimental, not adopted encyclopedia canon.

**Observation:** the first marker makes a case available off the road. Actual nearby
held recovery followed by a second held handoff can complete alongside the escort for
both approaches. Direct escort keeps the complete original Combat snapshot. Recovery
commits attention while threats/courier continue; a controlled pulse still damages the
interacting player. Moving/releasing cancels progress, pause freezes it and a carried
case at gate is never automatically reported delivered.

The fixed feasible Guardian route ends with courier2/3 versus3/3 direct; Duelist ends
both with3/3 and different counter histories. These are policy-specific outcomes, not
an unavoidable recovery tax, optimal strategies or comparative class balance. The first
route script repeatedly chased the moving courier, canceling handoff until both roles
lost. Sustaining E within actual handoff range corrected the script, not the combat
model. Presentation now draws that true range and distinguishes carrying from delivery.

**Interpretation/consequence:** a short world choice can use existing positional and
temporal commitments without requiring branches of an MMO world or a new reward ledger.
Expose the action's acceptance boundary, preserve main-objective success when declined
and describe the outcome actually executed; loss cannot imply a location never reached.

**Validity:** source/PCK routes, prepared OS-keyboard interaction and agent image review
are distinct evidence. One rendered Guardian baseline and recovery route also execute
one step per physics tick to completion in a bounded software-rendered environment;
this narrows the earlier timeout uncertainty without diagnosing its cause or establishing
all-device real-time performance. No human comprehension, identity, preference, ethical
judgment or fun is established. No P10 code, account progression or shared-world state
has been integrated. Revisit after independent player observation or new route policies.

## Recoverable keyboard preferences in the destination

**Question/context:** Can a player use alternate keyboard positions without changing
character-action commitment? **Source:** baseline `3b1eafc`,
[controls execution](../evidence/north-star-controls/RUN.json); ENG-022, SCI-061,
CRE-001/075/081. **Observation:** native source/PCK and separate-process controls pass;
actual X11 input confirms H guard and F medicine recovery, old-key rejection, conflict
recovery and preserved invalid profile bytes. Same-code synthetic events initially
missed physical/logical arrow divergence; an actual window exposed it, and a dedicated

input plus model checks; persistence needs validated numeric identity before readback.
**Validity limit:** one software-display keyboard setup, synthetic input and prepared
medicine positions; no disability cohort, ergonomic preference or comprehension result.
**Consequence:** keep management recovery reachable, drafts explicit, hints tied to the
applied map, and ability semantics independent of key selection. Hold/toggle alternatives
remain unresolved rather than implied accessible by remapping alone.

## Event sound needs temporal ownership, not historical replay

**Question/context:** Baseline `446ecd4` is silent despite actual defense and draw
choices. **Source:** [sound execution](../evidence/north-star-sound/RUN.json), original
P08/P06 bounded-cue method, ENG-005/023, SCI-064, CRE-001/067/073.
**Observation:** source/PCK produce distinct warning/response PCM through AudioServer;
historical PCK is silent in the same prepared exercises. Mute/pause probes are digitally
silent, accepted block/draw/counter and terminal events own their feedback. Attaching
to an advanced model initially replayed history; consuming its cursor and seeding new
attempts prevents it. A live guard still needs the original 60ms, regardless of sound.
**Interpretation/consequence:** observer attachment, pause, unmute and model replacement
are causal boundaries; do not turn a retrospective event log into present feedback.
Two bounded voices and silence leave warnings room without an unrelated music system.
**Validity:** software mixer output and signal properties, not physical listening,
recognition, comfort, accessibility or enjoyment. Mixer sample counts vary across runs;
compare deterministic event ownership and signal bounds, not whole capture hashes.

## An internal technique needs an executed exclusion and a real opportunity

**Question/context:** Can Guardian change defensive rhythm without an attribute tree?
**Source:** baseline e86b596, [technique execution](../evidence/north-star-guard-techniques/RUN.json),
ENG-005, SCI-017 and CRE-001/011/013/014. **Observation:** Steady held guard remains safe;
Riposte held too early expires and takes damage. Correct timing grants 1.6s instead of
1.0s OPEN, accepting an actual counter at 1.2s that Steady rejects. Whole normal and
medicine routes execute; naive early holding loses under the timed technique. Choice
is locked in attempts/rehearsal; retry preserves it but clears committed state.
**Interpretation:** an exclusion plus an observable response opportunity can express a
bounded build choice without a skill-tree framework, permanent stats or reward farming.
**Validity:** fixed policies and synthetic input, not player preference, balance or fun.

**Negative measurement finding:** PNG polling with per-tap waits during the critical interval observed
tick 51, then K reached baseline tick 59/current tick 60; both failed. A lightweight
consumer probe observed/received tick 45 and the intended response passed. The capture
method perturbed the timing under observation. **Consequence:** measure receipt in the
consumer and bound instrumentation overhead before blaming a new timing rule. This
comparison does not measure hardware latency or prove all rendered frame performance.

## Visible contact needs the same owner and clock as the action

**Question/context:** At baseline `2d32a61`, actual actions mostly retain static body
shapes, and a paid armor strike has no body gesture. **Source:**
[actor production](../evidence/north-star-action-poses/RUN.json), ENG-023, SCI-014,
CRE-067/068/074. **Observation:** new native actors use actual displacement and accepted
cooldown/event transitions. A spent armor strike animates without a contact mark; a
confirmed counter alone moves the sentinel. Courier damage belongs to its body, and
west-facing protection keeps its incoming-side mark. Simulation-time effects freeze
with pause/review; attaching to an advanced/replaced model cannot replay old contacts.
Matched rendered samples preserve combat values and source/PCK pixels in one renderer.

**Interpretation/consequence:** let model ownership determine what art may claim, while
keeping drawing read-only. Separate an accepted attempt from its successful contact.
Immediate model contact needs a contact/recovery gesture, not a newly invented windup.
Inspect at the actual viewport: labels/direction markers crossed the blade in the first
pass and were moved/shaped separately. **Validity:** these are scoped implementation
and agent creative observations, not player recognition, class identity, comfort,
accessibility, balance or real-time performance evidence. No reusable origin atlas was
extracted, no new lab created, and no source/reference rights were altered.
