# Consequential cooperation — a finite offer must reach actual play

**Question/context:** Can a short NPC exchange acknowledge performed actions and
change the next playable goal without becoming a detached results menu or a generic
narrative framework? Baseline `b4feb72076bbc78ee9128eb43ccbb8bb7bc69217` exposed route
and medicine labels after escort success, then immediately offered another combat goal.

**Sources:** [Actual conversation](../north-star/prototype/courier_talk.gd),
[destination execution](../evidence/north-star-courier-conversation/RUN.json),
[P10's inspected scene method](../projects/waystone-dialogue/scene.py), CRE-005 A,
CRE-041/048 H, ENG-005 and SCI-017. P10 checks availability before mutation and selects
responses from actual pre-turn facts. Its implementation/fiction is not imported.

**Observation:** The courier remembers actual route, response, shutdown and medicine
ownership. Selection alone, cancellation, information reading and interruption grant
nothing. A confirmed strip prepares either one capped health point or one winch
integrity, never both. Reopening/retry cannot multiply it. Eight matched complete
journeys hold the preceding wounded escort constant; declining and skipping have
identical mechanics. Separate fixture controls cover the three medicine-state adapters;
these are not three new full recovery journeys or independent participants.

**Interpretation:** A bounded local conversation can connect recognizable action
memory, a readable exclusive cost and the next executed objective. Explicit ownership
belongs to the prepared checkpoint, rather than a live heal or repeated dialogue entry.
Acknowledging an uncompleted handoff must not silently turn it into delivered medicine.

**Validity limit:** Fixed policies, synthetic native/OS input and agent inspection.
No player comprehension, emotional attachment, moral significance, perceived identity,
balance, preference, enjoyment or persistent social simulation has been validated.
The original courier/strip fiction remains experimental; no candidate canon is adopted.

**Practical consequence:** Keep preconditions, remembered facts, draft intent,
confirmation and consumed consequence separate. Verify the destination resource and
retry boundary, not only the dialogue label. Before enlarging the conversation or
adding reputation/persistence, investigate what actual players understand and value.
