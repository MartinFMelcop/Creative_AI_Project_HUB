# Selected round initiative — prospective acceptance protocol

Status: specified before guarded acceptance execution; exploratory native producer
runs preceded this final protocol. Changes made after observations remain identified
in retained results. This is an implementing-team software protocol, not external
preregistration or a study with people.

## Question and alternatives

Can selected preserved per-round initiative and visible-order requirements make the
same Treatment Line's first-contact opportunity consequential without advancing DOT,
duration, resources or rounds through reads, and without changing classic/response/
control modes? The alternative was targeted fizzle: precise source rules exist, but
this consumer has no real target invalidation mechanism. Another Field extension would
be a third consecutive product unit. General tooling has no established blocking need.

Beneficiary: the existing practice player deciding between a slower stronger and a
faster weaker preparation, and the combat laboratory resolving a bounded source duty.
This is original staging, not approved classes, fiction, economy or commercial clearance.

## Source versus destination

The preserved `REGRAS_DE_COMBATE.csv` supplies INI-001/002/004/005 (recalculate round
order, randomized tie resolution, visible timeline and bounded variation) plus FLW-002/
003/005 (one action per turn, living-start round boundary, selected end-round effects).
The assay binds literal rows and the full original file SHA-256. Historical DEV/DIST
combat helpers are inspected separately; no historical initiative runtime is claimed.
The source does not specify a numeric AGI formula, distribution, PRNG or seed.

Destination policies are ORIGINAL: two actors, one turn each per global round;
Heavy AGI8/strike32/bash18 or Quick AGI16/strike16/bash10; target AGI12; bounded jitter
[-6,+6]; fixed reproducible encounter seed and explicitly declared deterministic RNG.
The original numeric policy is uint32 LCG `state=state*1664525+1013904223`; player
draw then target draw, each modulo13 minus6; descending AGI+jitter score; equal score
uses one extra draw, low bit0 means player first. Same-input Python/C# controls bind
exact scores, order and final draw state. Source INI-002 generic order-tie RNG is
adapted to equal scores; this is not a recovered equal-AGI formula. No bonus turns (INI-003), formation,
full battle pipeline, SP regeneration, cooldown, hit RNG or historical formula recovery.

Classic mode, passive/response/control preparation and their existing fixed policies
remain regression obligations. No Crossing, Field, Godot or recipient package adaptation.

## Rival predictions and required controls

- Decorative timeline: compare actual before/after owner snapshots and accepted full
  policy. A faster first lethal action must prevent actual target contact; a slower
  action must pay contact first where still alive. Show an unchanged-tuning loss too.
- Fixed order disguised as randomness: bind round number, seed/draw state, actor AGI,
  bounded jitter/score/tie data and next order; independent same-input cases must include
  both orders and ties. Repeatability alone does not prove varying round order.
- Time/read manipulation: GET, idle time, reload and rejected commands preserve exact
  owner state and current queue. Only accepted actor turns advance the round cursor.
- Double round costs: one actor turn is not a global round. DOT/duration occurs once
  after the living-start actors have their turns, with terminal and killed-before-turn
  exceptions explicitly tested. No ghost action after defeat or duplicated last turn.
  Withdrawal happens in the actual player slot and closes no round or DOT; an earlier
  target slot may still hit or kill before withdrawal can occur. This exception is
  an original terminal policy, not a claim that every living-start actor always acts.
- Preparation as live swap: tempo only in explicit preparation, no active or ended
  swap/refund; reentry is an explicit new ephemeral encounter.
- False renderer: an actual isolated observer with a falsified queue must be detected
  by owner-bound DOM assertions; documentary hashes alone are insufficient.
- Unknown accepted receipt: delayed/dropped command receipts retain quarantine and
  require explicit read recovery without automatic replay or a second paid action.
