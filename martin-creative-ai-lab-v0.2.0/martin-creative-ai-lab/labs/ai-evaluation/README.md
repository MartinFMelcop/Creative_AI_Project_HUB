# AI Evaluation & Quality Laboratory

P02 owns this mandatory destination. Its bounded question is whether a calibrated
evaluator can distinguish defects in an independently produced client artifact from
an unavailable or contradictory execution environment. The beneficiary is the HUB
maintainer deciding whether to accept that artifact. Artifact acceptance is not a
model score, a North Star gameplay capability or independent human validation.

## Candidate context and frozen attempt

The candidate receives a fresh exported directory containing seven starter app files,
the brief and its manifest. Its agent is spawned without the evaluator conversation.
The permitted context/tools are declared before generation: local exported sources,
Node syntax checks or local application investigation, and edits only to the client.
Repository history, reference solutions, evaluator checks, network access and
dependency installation are excluded from the allowed investigation. Available
Node/npm/Chromium tooling does not imply permission to obtain additional context.
The candidate receives no evaluator
feedback before its first submitted artifact is frozen and hashed.

These are procedural context limits, not an operating-system sandbox. The shared
filesystem and generally capable tools do not enforce secrecy. The task and answer
history are public, and unknown prior model exposure cannot be excluded. Candidate
self-reports and observed calls must remain distinguishable from externally enforced
access controls. A separate context is independent artifact generation within this
declared procedure; it is not evidence of an unseen task, independent training,
another model family or confidential benchmarking. Do not infer model identity,
token usage or monetary cost when the platform does not provide them.

Freeze the first delivery before evaluator-owned checks. Retain its exact bytes,
identity, provided context, allowed tools, observed attempts and available timing.
Do not silently replace a rejected first attempt with a repaired artifact or feed
hidden-test findings back into an attempt described as independent. A repair can
only be a separately identified later intervention.

For the recorded execution, the complete protocol, including its candidate and
constraints fields, was finalized before candidate spawn. Its pre-spawn SHA-256 is
`abcfbb49d11adb85da076587dcb9f1e0b22a134e1e4f5c701bbf21ab6e5b1c1a`;
the later delivery-freeze record binds those same protocol bytes. The candidate's
reported access list remains a report checked against visible tool calls, not an
operating-system access audit.

## Refutable acceptance and rival explanations

Run the existing calibration in the same environment as the candidate:

```sh
python3 evaluations/chat-send-buffering/test_runner.py
python3 labs/ai-evaluation/test_instrument.py
python3 labs/ai-evaluation/run.py --protocol /tmp/ai-protocol.json --workspace /tmp/ai-candidate --output /tmp/ai-evaluation-run
```

Directories and result files must be fresh; outputs stay outside maintained sources.
The destination instrument validates explicit candidate attribution/tools, first-attempt
limits and the declared absence of a sandbox. It freezes the client before executing
fresh pinned baseline/reference/candidate exports, retains the exact protocol and
client, and binds the actual candidate result to that frozen identity. Its output
contains the three full origin records and a bounded decision record. It does not
launch an AI candidate or independently establish the truth of context declarations;
the external pre-spawn and delivery provenance is essential.

The baseline must be rejected with exactly 15 passed and 2 failed checks; the committed
reference must be accepted with 17 passed and0 failed. Both use17 complete tests with
zero cancelled, skipped or todo results. The actual pinned suite contains six
protocol and eleven browser checks. Calibration failure prevents an affirmative
candidate decision; it cannot be attributed to candidate behavior.

A candidate passes only with an intact submission scope, successful syntax and
environment gates, complete actual execution and17/17 passing checks. Preserve
individual failing check names and actual exit/timeout diagnostics. Invalid support
files or a definite client parser diagnostic can reject an artifact before runtime.
Missing dependencies/browser, launch errors, timeouts, incomplete capture and
contradictory TAP/exit summaries are inconclusive, not evidence of a candidate defect.
The runner's calibrated negatives exercise these distinctions; unexpected execution
failure must never count as successful detection of an injected defect.

The rival explanations are explicit: the client remains wrong; the evaluator or
environment fails; supplied context already exposed the solution; or the task's
finite checks miss another defect. Baseline/reference controls address only the
first two within their tested scope. Context records expose procedural contamination
risk but cannot establish absence of pretraining exposure. Passing17 checks does not
prove correctness under all networks or future changes.

## Selection, validity and next objective

Independent Engineering, Scientific and Creative analyses compared this incomplete
laboratory with Build/Operations, coherent authored C# integration and actual
participant/device assessment. Build/Operations remains consequential, but its
published Windows-delivery question needs a usable licensed Unity Editor and Windows
modules. A Linux host/package cannot satisfy it. Bringing the authored narrative's
reservation, departure and observed return into the audiovisual C# consumer remains
a strong product alternative. Participant/device assessment is the appropriate
method for comprehension, cue recognition and comfort; more synthetic media controls
cannot supply participants or physical devices.

This method uses existing real tools to close the narrower missing independent-context
artifact decision, rather than repeating isolated audio/atlas calibration. SCI-076
informed matching the method to the desired claim; SCI-077 informed preserving
negatives, contamination and environment uncertainty instead of equating a rubric,
hash or executable artifact with broader validity.

No population estimate, model comparison, causal productivity gain, human usability,
creative quality, production outage reliability, security sandbox, Unity, Windows or
Steam result follows. Existing actual consumers and protected sources remain intact.
Next: use the observed candidate outcome to decide whether the bounded acceptance
method is sufficient, requires additional discriminating cases or should be suspended.
Compare fresh independent tasks and real C# production decisions against the remaining
delivery and authored-integration obligations; do not turn this public practice task
into a permanent benchmark or endlessly repair candidates until they pass.

## Executed decision and reproduction

[Execution](../../evidence/ai-evaluation/RUN.json) binds the actual method and source
bytes; [results](../../evidence/ai-evaluation/results.json) binds complete calibration,
counts and the frozen artifact. The baseline rejects 15/17 on the exact polling-write

all 17 real checks, with no cancelled/skipped/todo outcomes. The candidate changed only
`public/client.js`; it was not copied into P11 or North Star. Its private Socket.IO
`_hasPingExpired` dependency limits future compatibility; finite checks do not make
that an independently supported API.

The [predeclared protocol](../../evidence/ai-evaluation/protocol.json),
[exact spawn instruction](../../evidence/ai-evaluation/candidate-instruction.txt),
[delivery/access disclosure](../../evidence/ai-evaluation/frozen.json) and

Access disclosure is candidate-reported plus visible orchestration, not an OS audit.

An actual [missing-browser control](../../evidence/ai-evaluation/missing-browser.json)
uses the pinned reference and produces `inconclusive` at the environment gate, before
behavior checks. Seven method regressions cover provenance, false isolation/retry,
wrong-defect calibration, support tampering, immutable candidate ownership, output
preservation and concurrent instrument source mutation. The unchanged origin runner's

The [superseded first evaluator execution](../../evidence/ai-evaluation/superseded.json)
is retained: the instrument was edited while loaded code ran, exposing its original
end-of-run source-hash race. It is not acceptance evidence. The corrected method
captures identities first, requires stable sources through completion and refuses a
controlled mutation as inconclusive. Final execution rechecks the same frozen artifact
without candidate feedback. A further [artifact replay](../../evidence/ai-evaluation/replay.json)
reproduces exact outcome/control/source identities. These are one candidate delivery
and three evaluator executions, including the superseded study and reproduction.
