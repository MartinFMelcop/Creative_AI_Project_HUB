## Authority and observation contract

The [C# evaluator](../../projects/eclipse-fair/PolicyEvaluation/Experiment.cs) owns no game
rules. Existing Game.Legal, Game.Apply and isolated Game.Fork produce all accepted
transitions, one-step legal previews, terminal rankings and winner sets. Policies receive
immutable participant/rule facts, current/next public forecasts and legal options with
actual fork consequences. Public forecasts are legitimate observations, not illicit
information. Seed, journal, policy roster and future terminal results are evaluator-only.
This is an API boundary for trusted local heuristics, not an enforced OS sandbox.

The candidate is a deterministic authored route heuristic, not a trained model. The
collector baseline reproduces existing collector decisions under native parity controls.
Scoring preferences are policies; they cannot change authoritative prices, wages,
resource conservation, theft, terminal conditions or results.

## Frozen finite experiment

[Protocol](../../evidence/commander-policy/protocol.json) and
[configuration](../../evidence/commander-policy/config.json) predeclare baseline/candidate,
two scenarios, primary/held-out opponent rosters, eight seeds, four focal seats and three
cyclic opponent orders. Every focal assignment is one complete multiplayer game, not
four independent seat trials. Baseline/candidate games are paired by full configuration.
Rotations sharing seed/scenario remain dependent. Seed affects initial position,
initiative and events jointly; no isolated first-player causal effect is identified.

Report exclusive focal winners, multiwinner games, focal winner membership and fractional
credit separately; each of k winners receives1/k. Interrupted games have no fabricated
winner or terminal reason and remain in explicit accounting. Descriptive paired deltas
answer only the finite suite. No population confidence interval, human enjoyment,
Commander win rate, real card causality or automatic25% target is inferred.
