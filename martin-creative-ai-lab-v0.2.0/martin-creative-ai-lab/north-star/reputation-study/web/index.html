01 / CUSTODY

Take responsibility.

Borrow its available tool. It remains owed to this store; borrowing spends no time and changes no standing.

02 / OBSERVED RETURN

Bring that tool back.

Return the tool actually in your custody. A completed return costs two time and applies a +2 standing change here.

03 / SMALL CONTRIBUTION

Help with local work.

One time applies +1 under the selected source rule. At −1, this small change leaves standing at −1. Repeating help cannot cross that boundary.

04 / SERVICE

Pay for an available service.

This study requires standing of at least +1, two coins and one time. Only the accepted service creates its receipt; each store provides it once.
