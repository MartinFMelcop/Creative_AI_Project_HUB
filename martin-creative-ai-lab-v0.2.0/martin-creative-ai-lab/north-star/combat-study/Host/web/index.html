The Treatment Line

One target. Two treatment charges. Strike, bash, guard, treat and cleanse resolve one global round when accepted. Withdrawal ends practice immediately. Time and redraw never act for you.

Burn reduces attack to 70%; bleed reduces healing to 70%. Disarm blocks strike, but leaves bash available. Treat and cleanse each spend one charge and your action. Guard never reduces end-of-round DOT.
