# The kiln window — from an acquired repair to observed watering

One original continuous C# journey now makes the workshop's actual repair consequential.
Mara still has two pots of her own waiting. A fired repair costs three preparation phases;
a personal patch costs two. The fast approach costs one additional phase and two jar
integrity; the sheltered approach costs two phases with no initial jar wear. The whole
local horizon is four phases. Zero integrity means a broken jar and lost water.

You must actually acquire the repair, carry two water units through the real two-stage
Crossing, and explicitly pour one unit into each of two original seedbeds. The journey
privately composes the unchanged workshop and Crossing owners. The browser cannot submit
an invented repair, mark a threat won or declare watering. Work ready at Mara's yard is
not a collected jar; surviving the survey is not final arrival; arrival is not watering.

## Execute

Use SDK10.0.401 from the existing `north-star/csharp` pin. From the maintained workspace:

```sh
(cd north-star/csharp && dotnet build ../availability-study/Host/NorthStar.AvailabilityHost.csproj -c Release -m:1 --disable-build-servers)
dotnet north-star/availability-study/Host/bin/Release/net10.0/NorthStar.AvailabilityHost.dll --serve 8769 north-star/availability-study/Host/web --seconds 300
```

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8769/`. The bounded loopback host lasts1–600seconds; stop it explicitly
when finished. Substitute the actual SDK path if it is outside PATH. No GET, redraw,
promise, elapsed wall time or audio acts. Restart replaces the entire ephemeral attempt;
it is not saving or a free retry inside a journey. Uncertain responses require an explicit
read and never replay intent. This trusted-local observer is not an authenticated service.

The portable workshop rules remain C#8/netstandard2.1. The coordinator/host references
the existing .NET10 Crossing executable and Domain projects, so the composition is
**.NET10**, not a Unity-compatible/netstandard library or Unity/Windows delivery.

## Actual choices and authority

- Fired repair: reserve, shape, collect, then fast approach. Preparation3 + route1 fits
  the horizon; initial integrity3 pays wear2. A later collected repair at phase4 cannot
  invent a remaining travel phase.
- Personal patch: actual independent repair, then sheltered approach. Preparation2 +
  route2 fits; integrity1 survives departure. Taking the rough fast approach breaks it
  immediately and does not refund its paid phase.
- Both intact approaches begin the actual fixed Lure/Trail Crossing. Its own HP, resolve,
  accepted actions, XP and two encounters retain their unchanged owner. Each accepted
  positive HP-loss event consumes one jar integrity, independently of the damage amount.
  Route cost is already paid; encounter beats are not converted to workshop phases.
- Actual survey completion permits explicit continuation into the second encounter.
  Cargo persists across that boundary. Actual final completion with surviving water
  permits explicit watering. Each bed flag changes only when its water unit is poured;
  a keeper's receipt or future promise would not establish watering.

The current viable approaches each enter with one integrity. A damaging contact breaks
that cargo even if the child encounter is still active or has just won its survey. The
coordinator records broken cargo without inventing child defeat or auto-resetting it.
The defensive child-failure branch is not claimed as an independently reached outcome
under these fixed routes. This is an authored cost/impact convention, not material physics,
plant health, human balance or a claim that stronger preparation always solves the journey.

Mara's two pending pots and last workshop observation remain visible after departure as
historical facts. The road does not provide a fresh observation of her location or return.
Leaving a reservation outstanding can still make her unaccounted; advertised dawn cannot
restore her. No jar, NPC, phase or resource is refunded when a journey fails.
