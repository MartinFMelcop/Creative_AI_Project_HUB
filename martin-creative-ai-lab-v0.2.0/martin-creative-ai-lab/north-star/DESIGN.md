## Current product contract

The player chooses one experimental approach, escorts the courier to the far gate,
responds to a locked bolt and a ground pulse, and sees success or loss. Guard drains
posture, locks facing and slows movement; impact consumes more. Feint redirects an
eligible warning within range and spends resolve; movement/step must then leave its
locked line. A step changes position without invulnerability. Both can walk, strike,
pause, retry and change approach. A reachable OPEN counter delays the next threat.

Optional **T** rehearsal precedes or can be skipped for the same crossing. Enter arms
one trial; contact or missed opening freezes a review. Enter retries failure or advances
success, T repeats, and R abandons outside live play. No success is inferred from a
button press: block, draw-and-leave, pulse avoidance and reachable counter use actual
model outcomes. Each exercise resets its controlled setup. Completion starts the normal
crossing at full initial state, never with a training bonus. The last contact and its
recovery tip survive warnings and terminal text; opening range/time are visible.

## Evidence boundary and next selection

Native deterministic policies demonstrate feasible distinct completion, negative
controls and actual scene consumption. Rendered controlled journeys and synthetic
keyboard input establish scoped execution/presentation, not unassisted human learning,
identity perception, fun, accessibility or balance. No multiplayer, server, account,
persistent progression, class taxonomy approval or commercial evidence exists.

Each cycle must report observable product capability, relevant laboratory knowledge,
portfolio demonstration and origin migration. Preserve useful origins/consumers/tests;
close or consolidate obsolete obligations when evidence supports it. Reassess the next
weakness in the actual consumer rather than adding unrelated laboratory structures.
