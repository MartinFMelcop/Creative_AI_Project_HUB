## Keyboard-control decision — experimental

CRE-001 supplies direct author agency direction; CRE-081 documents remapping and
accessibility direction, while CRE-075 is a contextual-HUD hypothesis. The accepted
operational scope is a reversible local keyboard preference, not a change to final
classes, lore or combat balance. Twelve unique gameplay bindings, matching live hints,
explicit draft/apply/discard/default recovery and paused entry preserve deliberate
choices. Fixed management keys keep recovery reachable. The former toggle-hold deferral is superseded by the separately evaluated session
input alternatives below; it was not permission to change combat commitment. Synthetic/native
checks establish execution, not independent player understanding or accessibility.

## Sound-signature decision — experimental

CRE-001 supplies direct author character-agency direction. CRE-067/073 are hypotheses,
not approved class art or accessibility results. The previously silent encounter now
has three high bolt taps, a low rough floor pair, resonant block and airy draw; a dry
counter, damage thud and terminal cadences close the action loop. No ambient music or
footstep layer competes with warnings. Sound follows accepted events, not button presses.
Two voices bound overlap; terminal response has priority. Visual geometry/text remain
available, and F1/F5 mutes immediately for the session. No spatial or physical-device
claim follows. Recognition, mix comfort and experiential value require listening and
player observation; signal integrity alone cannot establish them.

## Guardian technique choice — experimental

CRE-001 is direct author identity direction. CRE-011/013/014 are build, exclusion and
Guardian hypotheses; implementation does not adopt their candidate trees or class canon.
Steady retains baseline sustained frontal guard and 1.0s OPEN. Title-only Tab selects
Riposte: guard age 60–300ms, then expiry while held, with 1.6s OPEN on a confirmed block.
The player trades sustained safety for timing commitment and extra counter opportunity;
there is no added stat tier, persistent power, special loot or default difficulty change.
Both keep posture, facing, floor vulnerability and actual 130px counter reach.

The selected technique is fixed through crossing, pause and rehearsal. Reentry through
R permits an explicit choice; a fresh process uses Steady. Rehearsal repeats retain choice
but reset commitment/resources. Its final prepared opening stays 1.0s and grants no buff.
Visible warning timing, guard state/expiry and retained failure cause make the trade-off
inspectable. A1.2s late counter is accepted only after the longer opening. Full routes
and synthetic OS input show execution, not balance, comprehension, preference or fun.

## Single-press input alternatives — experimental

Primary DEVELOPMENT, category 1. ENG-022 supplies predictable state and recoverability;
SCI-061 bounds accessibility claims to the actual population/context tested; CRE-081
(A) supports removing input barriers without hiding challenge, and CRE-005 (A) preserves
real choice/cost. These principles do not approve specific final control design.

F1/H and F1/I opt into session-only guard/interaction toggles independently. Default
hold and saved remaps remain. Guard toggles preserve drain, facing, startup and Riposte
expiry; Duelist draw stays discrete. Nearby stationary interaction preserves time,
range and no-defense commitment, disarms after completion and needs a separate handoff
press. Movement, range exit or another press cancels. Out-of-range presses cannot queue
future actions; guard pressed during recovery cannot queue a later raise.

Pause/focus/settings/terminal transitions and replacement clear active intentions;
resuming/retrying never re-arms them. Chosen modes persist only in this process.
Rejected: automatic re-raise after Riposte expiry, automatic handoff on proximity,
widened defense windows or silent profile-schema changes. These would change challenge
or introduce unintended actions beyond the input alternative. Actual native state,
source/PCK OS input and image inspection establish execution, not human validation.

## Roadside fitting — experimental

Primary DEVELOPMENT, category 1. CRE-022/023 are creative hypotheses: a small
profession activity needs a discernible trade-off, not repeated XP or a mandatory
minigame. The current author identity direction informs this bounded craft-to-build
consumer; neither candidate recipes nor profession taxonomy become canon.

One reusable blank can become a Riveted coat (6 health, 20% less walk/guard/step
mobility) or Trail wrap (4 health, 20% more mobility), for either approach. Standard
clothing stays 5 health at baseline speed. Walk to a real bench; hold interaction
for one uninterrupted second. Making spends once and visibly wears the piece. C
recycles only in preparation; partial work, settings/focus interruption and pause
cannot spend or produce an item. Held completion keeps its positive confirmation.

The fitting is locked into a clean normal crossing, including retry, and never
heals through a live swap. No courier bonus, new guard immunity or changed resource
price is granted. Practice remains isolated standard equipment. One process owns
this reversible choice; no inventory economy, profession progression or saved power.
ENG-005 informs conservation/state guards; SCI-017 informs unchanged-standard controls,
six complete policies and damage/movement checks. These are execution observations,
not class balance, professional identity, social recognition or player enjoyment.

Alternatives deferred: more timing refinement repeats established origin capability;
independent comprehension research needs actual participants; a persistent crafting
career would add storage/economy before testing whether this choice matters in play.
Review after observers can explain and choose the actual trade-off. No participant
study or current author acceptance of either item has occurred.

## Current product contract

The player chooses one experimental approach, escorts the courier to the far gate,
responds to a locked bolt and a ground pulse, and sees success or loss. Guard drains
posture, locks facing and slows movement; impact consumes more. Feint redirects an
eligible warning within range and spends resolve; movement/step must then leave its
locked line. A step changes position without invulnerability. Both can walk, strike,
pause, retry and change approach. A reachable OPEN counter delays the next threat and removes one of three focus plates.
The third disables this attempt's sentinel, cancels its pending attack and makes the
courier run at 56px/s rather than 28px/s. Survival and gate arrival still own success.

Optional **T** rehearsal precedes or can be skipped for the same crossing. Enter arms
one trial; contact or missed opening freezes a review. Enter retries failure or advances
success, T repeats, and R abandons outside live play. No success is inferred from a
button press: block, draw-and-leave, pulse avoidance and reachable counter use actual
model outcomes. Each exercise resets its controlled setup. Completion starts the normal
crossing at full initial state, never with a training bonus. The last contact and its
recovery tip survive warnings and terminal text; opening range/time are visible.

**Held the Line** requires a completed normal Guardian crossing with at least one
frontal block. **Drew the Danger** requires a completed normal Duelist crossing with
at least one drawn bolt. Both require player and courier survival; rehearsal, defeat
and abandonment award nothing. Each mark is earned once, never automatically worn.
**C**, outside live play and rehearsal, cycles none, owned Held the Line, owned Drew
the Danger in that order. The worn mark is drawn on the character and named in the
HUD; it changes no combat rule, resource, collision or result.

The editable JSON profile stores only owned marks and the chosen appearance on this
device. **F5**, from title/result/pause, retries a pending save. An unavailable profile
is preserved and blocks replacement while the current session remains playable.
After external restoration or removal, retry combines known marks with the accepted
profile and respects an explicitly changed appearance. This is bounded local cosmetic
continuity, not a selected account, server-authority or MMORPG persistence design.

A case falls south of the first marker once the courier reaches it. Optional recovery
requires 0.8s within 36px, followed by an actual 0.6s handoff within 54px of the moving
courier. Nearby eligible E suppresses combat actions, not simulation. Movement/release
cancel partial work; pause freezes it. The handoff ring and progress arc expose actual
acceptance boundaries. Only a won crossing with a completed handoff confirms safe delivery;
a carried case or defeat receives different text. No new persistent state is recorded.

controls and actual scene consumption. Rendered controlled journeys and synthetic
